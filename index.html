حكايتنا

Our story

In Arabic, bunn, بُن, is the word for ground coffee. So Nile & Bean is النيل والبُن: the river and the coffee.

The idea is simple: ordering should feel as warm and personal as walking into your favourite ahwa, where they already know how you take your coffee.
