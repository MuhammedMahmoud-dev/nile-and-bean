About Nile & Bean

In Arabic, bunn, بُن, is ground coffee. So Nile & Bean is النيل والبُن: the river, and the coffee.

The idea is simple. Ordering a coffee should feel as warm and personal as walking into your favourite ahwa, where they already know how you take it: sada, 'al-reeha, mazboot or ziyada.

What we care about

  • Made to orderEvery drink is built the way you set it, down to the sugar level.
  • The price you seeWhat the board shows is what your order costs. No surprises at the counter.
  • Pickup or deliveryCollect it, or have it brought to you.

Nile & Bean is a portfolio project. There is no real café behind it, and no order is made or delivered.
